On the **Curate** page of studio (`http://localhost:8730/curate`), convert the demonstrations gathered in [Demonstration collection](../collect/) into a LeRobot dataset. Conversion draws the robot's arms and the pegboard into every demonstration's gripper videos.

## Stages

The page has two stages, from top to bottom.

1. **Robot drawn**: See one frame of a demonstration that passed the verdict with the robot drawn in. It is the same picture conversion draws.
2. **Convert**: Convert the demonstrations that passed the verdict into a LeRobot dataset, the robot drawn into every frame of each.

## Why the robot is drawn in

The policy sees only the two gripper cameras' videos. While a demonstration is recorded those videos show the arm of the person holding the Hermit and the room; when the policy runs, the same cameras show the robot's arms and the pegboard they hang on. Trained on the demonstrations alone, a policy would be shown pictures at run time it never saw. So the robot is drawn into every demonstration video where it would stand, bringing the pictures it learns from close to the pictures it runs on.

## How it is drawn

| Item | How |
|---|---|
| Where the arms stand | The demonstrator's chest is taken as the robot's board ([role of the table marker](../../deep/teleoperation/#role-of-the-table-marker)) and the hands the top camera measured are carried into the robot's world. The first frame is solved by IK from the ready pose and each one after follows from the frame before, the same way the Collect page's replay places the arms |
| What is drawn | Every link of both arms below the wrist, the bases, and the scene's pegboard with its slots. The grippers (claw, jaw, servo, camera bracket) are left as the recorded Hermit shows them |
| Camera | Drawn from where the hand held that gripper camera at that moment, through the Kannala-Brandt lens the demonstration recorded. The 200° fisheye sees behind the plane of its lens, and nothing is drawn outside the lens circle |
| Colour | The printed parts take the pink measured off the other hand's Hermit in the same frame, so they follow the room's light. Frames where the other Hermit is not seen, the servos and the pegboard use the `robot_drawn_*` colours in the UMI settings |
| What it covers | What is drawn lies over whatever the frame shows there - the person's arm, the Hermit's marker, the room - except the two grippers |

A demonstration the robot cannot be drawn into is left out of the conversion, and the progress lines say why.

- It is not a two-hand demonstration
- It was recorded before the table marker was placed at the chest, so it cannot be carried into the robot's world
- One hand was never located from start to end

The gripper videos of a [rollout recording](../../deep/corrections/) with corrections come from the robot's own cameras and already show the robot, so nothing is drawn into them.

## Preview

In **Robot drawn**, pick a demonstration, the Left or Right camera and a frame to see that frame with the robot drawn in. The first frame of a demonstration takes a few seconds while the arms are followed through the whole of it; other frames of the same demonstration come at once.

## Convert

Enter a dataset name and a task description in the **Convert** stage and press **Convert** to convert every demonstration that passed the verdict. Demonstrations that failed are left out even if they were not deleted, and the progress lines say why. Write the task description as an **English sentence** describing the demonstrated task (for example `pick and place the block`). The dataset is saved to `~/pinkrab/datasets/<name>/` and appears in the Datasets list.

Conversion settings are not chosen separately.

- **Robot**: Drawn into every demonstration. A demonstration takes about a minute.
- **fps**: The core setting `dataset_fps` (50), and a trained policy **runs at this rate**. How the gaps between camera frames are filled is described in [Dataset fps](../../deep/convert/#dataset-fps).

When conversion finishes, open the dataset with **View the dataset** under the progress lines, or with **View** on its row in the Datasets list. The episodes and the demonstrations they came from are listed on the left, and the two gripper videos play exactly as stored in the dataset. These are the pictures training reads.

The same conversion can also be done from the command line. The command converts the listed demonstrations as they are, without the verdict, and draws the robot in with `--robot-drawn`.

```bash
pinkrab-umi convert --robot-drawn --out pick_place --task "pick and place the block" <episode> <episode> ...
```
