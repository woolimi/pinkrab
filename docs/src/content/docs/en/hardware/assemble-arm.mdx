One arm is assembled from printed parts, 7 STS3215 servos, one servo board, and one camera. From the base to the fingertip, you alternate between a servo and the part that servo turns, and the servo ids are **1–7 starting from the base**. The left and right arms are mirror images of each other, so the order is the same.

<Assembly kind="arm" />

- Servo ids are set after assembly, in [Setup](../../use/setup/#servo-id), by **connecting the servos one at a time**.

## Screws

The count is the sum of the screws used in each step.
