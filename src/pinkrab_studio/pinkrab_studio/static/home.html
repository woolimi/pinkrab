    Collect

    Record episodes with the handheld gripper: its cameras, the top camera's view of the solid and the desk, the pedals. No robot needed.

     

     2

    Curate

    Keep the takes worth training on, label what each gripper picture keeps, look the labels over, and convert - as recorded or with randomised backgrounds.

     

     3

    Train

    Train on this machine or a GPU machine over ssh, and watch the loss come down as it does.

     

     4

    Policy

    Show a checkpoint what the gripper camera sees now, see what it asks for, then let it drive an arm.
